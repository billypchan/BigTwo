# Big Two — Privacy Policy

Big Two itself asks for nothing about you: no account, no sign-in, no analytics.

- Your preferences (rule set, autopass, score display) are saved only on your device,
  in the app's own settings, and are removed when you delete the app.
- The game record (open hands and each step) is saved only on your device, and is
  removed when you delete the app.
- Nothing you do in the game is sent anywhere by us.

## Advertising

The game shows a banner advertisement, served by **Google AdMob**. To do that, the
Google Mobile Ads SDK connects to Google and may read and send device information —
such as a device advertising identifier, coarse location derived from your IP address,
and how the ad performed. That data goes to Google, not to us; we never see it and we
receive no personal data from it.

- Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Where required (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), a consent form appears before any
  ad is requested, and your answer decides whether ads are personalised. Nothing is
  requested before you have answered.
- Big Two does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so ads are not
  personalised using data collected across other companies' apps and websites.
